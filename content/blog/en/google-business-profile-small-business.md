When a customer searches for "electrician Toronto" or "restaurant near me", Google first shows a map and three businesses. That block comes from **Google Business Profile**. It is free, and for many small businesses it is the number one source of new customers.

Here are the 8 settings that make the difference.

## 1. The right primary category

This is the most important setting. Choose the **most precise** category available: "Chartered accountant" rather than "Accountant" if that fits. Then add a few secondary categories for your other activities, without overdoing it.

## 2. Address or service area

- Customers come to you: show your **address**.
- You go to them (trades, repairs, home services): hide your address and set a **service area** (cities or regions).

Google [states it clearly](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en): the distance between the business and the searcher is one of the three local ranking factors, along with relevance and prominence.

## 3. A name with no added keywords

Your profile name must be **your real business name**. Adding "– Cheap Plumber Toronto" breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.

## 4. Accurate hours, including holidays

A customer who shows up to a closed door won't come back, and may leave a bad review. Update special hours for holidays, summer breaks and closures.

## 5. Real, recent photos

Storefront (so people recognize you), interior, team, completed work. Avoid stock photos. Add new ones regularly: an active profile builds trust.

## 6. Services and description

Fill in your **list of services** and a clear description: what you do, for whom, where. Write for your customers, in their words, without stuffing keywords.

## 7. A steady flow of reviews, and replies

Reviews count for rankings as much as for the customer's decision. Ask for them systematically after a successful job, using the direct link Google provides. **Reply to every one**, negative reviews included, calmly and personally.

Buying or swapping reviews breaks Google's policies, and fake reviews can breach consumer protection law.

## 8. Regular posts

News, offers, events, recent projects: posts show the business is active and keep the profile fresh.

## What about the website?

The profile and the website reinforce each other. Your website should show **exactly** the same name, address and phone number as the profile, and ideally one page per city you serve. That is what helps a profile move up into the three businesses shown first on the map.

## In practice

Setting up a profile properly takes a few hours, then a few minutes a week to maintain. It is one of the most cost-effective SEO investments for a local business.
