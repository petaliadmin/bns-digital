Your customers no longer rely on Google alone. They ask ChatGPT "which agency should build my website?" or Perplexity "what invoicing software suits a contractor?". **GEO** (*Generative Engine Optimization*) aims to get your business **cited in those answers**.

The good news: GEO largely builds on SEO fundamentals. Here are the concrete levers.

## 1. Let AI crawlers read your website

Assistants that search the web use their own crawlers. OpenAI [documents its bots](https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots) (including `OAI-SearchBot`, used for ChatGPT search), as does [Perplexity](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots) (`PerplexityBot`).

Check that your `robots.txt` file doesn't block them. Some hosts and firewalls block these bots by default without telling you.

## 2. Be on Bing, not just Google

Several assistants rely on third-party search indexes, including Bing's, and Microsoft Copilot is built directly on it. Add your website to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) and submit your sitemap: it is free and quick.

## 3. Clear, quotable answers

AI assistants extract **passages**. A paragraph that answers a question precisely is more likely to be quoted than a long, vague text.

- Ask the question in a heading ("How long does it take to build a website?").
- Answer in the first sentence, then go into detail.
- Use lists and tables for steps and comparisons.
- An FAQ on each service page is an ideal format.

## 4. Factual, verifiable information

AI favours precise sources: who you are, where you are, what you do, for whom, since when. Avoid empty phrases ("innovative tailored solutions") in favour of facts: detailed services, areas served, methods, terms.

## 5. Structured data

Schema.org markup (`Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`) describes your business in a format machines read without ambiguity. It pays off for Google and AI alike.

## 6. An llms.txt file (optional)

`llms.txt` is a proposed format: a Markdown summary of your website, at the root of your domain, meant for language models. It is not an official standard and Google says it doesn't use it, but it is simple to set up. Our website has one.

## 7. Get mentioned beyond your own website

To recommend a business, an AI relies on what is said **about it**: business directories, press articles, comparisons, customer reviews, LinkedIn profiles. The more consistently your brand is mentioned on reliable websites, the more likely it is to appear.

## Measuring GEO

There is no Search Console for AI yet. The simplest approach: regularly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask, and note whether you are cited. Also watch your analytics for visits from `chatgpt.com` or `perplexity.ai`.

## In practice

GEO doesn't replace SEO: it extends it. A technically sound website, precise content and a brand present across the web are the best assets, for Google and AI alike.
